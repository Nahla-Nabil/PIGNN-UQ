\section{Introduction}

\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{Voluntary carbon markets discount mangrove credits for }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{Under Verra's VM0033 methodology, a mangrove project deposits part of its credits in a pooled buffer account against }\DIFaddend non-permanence risk (carbon later released \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{via }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{through }\DIFaddend erosion, sea-level rise \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{, }\DIFdelend or disturbance)\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{using a buffer pool: a fixed percentage of issued credits withheld from sale, unconditioned on how confident the underlying carbon-stock estimate
is.
Verra's VM0033 and Plan Vivo V5.0 both apply this uniformly, regardless of sub-project granularity
\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{verra_vm0033,planvivo_buffer}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
, and existing carbon-stock models for
this ecosystem and region report only point estimates, with no
calibrated per-prediction confidence a pricing rule could condition on
\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{radabaugh2023bluecarbon,sanderman2023soc,simard2019mangrove}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
.
Conformal prediction supplies valid, distribution-free uncertainty for
earth-observation and spatial regression
\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{eo_conformal2024,lou2024geoconformal,gonzalezabad2023ensembles}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
,
but no existing work pairs that calibrated uncertainty with a physics-informed spatial predictor, couples it to a buffer-pool pricing
mechanism, or tests whether a calibration strategy (global versus
spatially localized) holds under one region's calibration-density
constraints, the narrower gap this paper addresses (Section~II surveys
each literature in full). This paper }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{, and a separate deduction applies when the 90\% confidence interval of the estimate exceeds 20\% of its value \mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite[p.~64]{verra_vm0033}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
.
The buffer share comes from a risk assessment and does not depend on how well the carbon stock itself was estimated.
Published mangrove carbon maps either report no per-site interval or report intervals whose coverage and crediting effect have not been checked \mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{maxwell2023soc,radabaugh2023bluecarbon}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
, and the methodology combines plot uncertainties under an independence assumption that spatial models rarely satisfy.
}

\DIFadd{This paper asks whether calibrated, per-site uncertainty from a spatial model can supply that deduction in a way a registry could audit.
The proposed pipeline, PIGNN-UQ (Fig.~\ref{fig:framework}), }\DIFaddend combines a physics-informed graph neural network \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{raissi2019pinn,sanchezgonzalez2020gns}}\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
, previously
applied to mass-conserving flood forecasting
\mbox{%DIFAUXCMD
\cite{taghizadeh2025floodpignn,dualfloodgnn2025} }\hskip0pt%DIFAUXCMD
but not, to our
knowledge, blue carbon, with distribution-free uncertainty calibration
to test whether estimation confidence can complement, not replace, a
flat }\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{(GNN) that encodes neighborhood structure and a tidal-inundation prior, tree ensembles as strong tabular baselines, and conformalized quantile regression for intervals calibrated on held-out data.
This study departs from earlier work in four ways: (1) the interval of every node is converted into the VM0033 deduction, kept separate from the }\DIFaddend non-permanence \DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{discount.
}\DIFdelend \DIFaddbegin \DIFadd{buffer and compared with the buffers held by issuing projects; (2) the methodology's own aggregation rule is tested empirically and shown to understate project-level uncertainty when model errors are spatially correlated; (3) predictions and published maps are checked against 87 independent field soil cores; and (4) the analysis covers two regions, two image dates and leakage-free spatial splits, with model choices fixed on validation data under rules committed in advance.
}

\DIFadd{The scope is deliberately applied: the paper does not propose a new network architecture.
The contribution is a crediting pipeline whose uncertainty is calibrated, auditable and conservative, together with evidence on where that uncertainty is and is not informative.
Table~\ref{tab:gap} positions this contribution against prior work.
}\DIFaddend 

\DIFdelbegin \DIFdel{The scope is deliberately narrow: one pilot region, one physics constraint
(a steady-state mass-balance proxy, not a full temporal flux equation), and one primary UQ method (CQR; deep ensembles and MC dropout are left
for future work ). Every model-selection decision, physics-loss weight,
feature set, calibration method, was pre-registered on a validation
split and confirmed once on a spatially distinct test block never used
for any prior decision; we treat this two-stage protocol itself as a
methodological contribution (Section~III-C) , since a naive single-run
estimate and a theoretically-motivated calibration refinement each turned
out to mislead here (Section~V) . What survives that scrutiny is smaller
and more tightly bounded than either initial result suggested: a real
but modest prior-generalization advantage for the physics-informed model,
and evidence that localized (Mondrian) conformal calibration, plausible
in theory and competitive on validation-internal evidence, underperforms
the simpler global variant on this AOI's test data . The contribution is
this pre-registered evaluation and its honest limits, not a validated
point-prediction accuracy claim or a validated pricing model.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol}
\DIFadd{Feature sets, model settings, the physics prior and the interval method were chosen by spatial five-fold cross-validation (CV) on training and validation nodes, using selection rules written and committed before each experiment.
The test split was read once for all pre-specified models.
After that read, one further model (the ensemble) was pre-specified on cross-validation alone and evaluated in a second, declared test read, and its test numbers are marked in Table~\ref{tab:acc}.
Analyses that followed the first test read (generalization, two-date training, project aggregation, field cores, market data) used validation data or external data only and are reported as exploratory.
Nine logged deviations and all rules are archived with the code}\DIFaddend .
